The Eclipse philosophy

Our Story

Eclipse was founded by Laziz Uktamov on 21.11.2026. It began with a simple idea: everyone deserves the confidence to follow their own path.

Laziz Uktamov is pursuing his dream of becoming a professional soccer player.

In a world where people are often told who to be, Eclipse stands for individuality, ambition, and self-expression. Just as an eclipse is a rare and powerful moment that captures everyone's attention, every person has the potential to create their own unique impact on the world.

Our mission is to design premium clothing that inspires people to stand out, chase their goals, and find their own orbit. Every piece is made with comfort, quality, and timeless style in mind, using 100% cotton fabrics that feel as good as they look.

Eclipse is more than a clothing brand. It is a community of dreamers, creators, athletes, artists, students, and entrepreneurs who believe that age, background, or circumstances should never limit what they can achieve.

We believe greatness begins when you stop following someone else's path and start creating your own.
